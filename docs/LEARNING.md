# Teaching agreement

## Session protocol

1. Present a concrete problem before naming the technique.
2. Ask for a prediction and its reasoning.
3. Build a visual model with labelled quantities, axes and units.
4. Work with small numbers by hand.
5. Derive the relationship; introduce notation after its meaning.
6. Ask what assumptions support it and construct a counterexample.
7. Use a small experiment to check a prediction.
8. Ask the learner to explain back and apply the idea elsewhere.
9. Revisit with a delayed, unlabelled problem.

Use diagrams, geometric transformations, plots and tables where useful. A visual must show a relationship, not merely decorate a lesson.

## Help policy

Allow a genuine attempt before showing a solution. Use hints in order:
- Ask a question that exposes the missing step.
- Suggest a representation or simpler case.
- Reveal one intermediate step.
- Give a worked solution only when useful, then require reconstruction from memory.

If an explanation goes over the learner's head, identify and teach the missing prerequisite. Do not keep paraphrasing the same advanced explanation.

## Difficulty ladder

| Level | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 1: calculation | Correct operation with units and shapes |
| 2: explanation | Meaning of the operation and result |
| 3: derivation | Reconstruct a rule with assumptions |
| 4: application | Translate a situation into mathematics |
| 5: changed assumptions | Diagnose failure and construct counterexamples |
| 6: transfer | Select and use the idea in an unfamiliar setting |

Different difficulty means different reasoning demands, not just larger numbers. Include short justifications and small proofs. Use mixed problems without topic labels.

## Mastery

See ASSESSMENT.md. One easy answer or a green numerical check is insufficient. Separate arithmetic slips from mistaken concepts, missing prerequisites, poor problem formulation and unjustified assumptions.

Record mistakes as:
- What I believed.
- Evidence that contradicted it.
- Missing concept or assumption.
- Revised explanation.
- A fresh problem that distinguishes the two models.

## AI use

AI may ask questions, generate variations, draw explanations, critique reasoning and check a completed derivation. During independent checks, attempt without hints or generated solutions. State assistance in the evidence log. Reconstruct any studied solution later with no reference.

## Session closure

Record: where am I standing, what am I trying to move, what mathematical tool helps, why it works and what could make it fail. Finish with one application and one unresolved question.
