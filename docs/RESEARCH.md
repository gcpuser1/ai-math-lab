# Resource review

Reviewed 22 October 2026 through official course/author pages. This is a curated syllabus comparison, not a systematic review of every resource. Resource availability and individual course logistics can change.

## Selection

| Source | Role | Fit and limits |
|---|---|---|
| [3Blue1Brown](https://www.3blue1brown.com/) | Visual entry points in linear algebra/calculus/neural networks | Build intuition; follow with derivations and independent problems |
| [MIT 18.06SC](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-06sc-linear-algebra-fall-2011/) | Linear algebra lessons, problem-solving material, assignments and exams | Main selected exercise source; not the entire course in 25 sessions |
| [MIT 18.01SC](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-01sc-single-variable-calculus-fall-2010/) | Single-variable calculus | Select limits, differentiation and basic integration |
| [MIT 18.02SC](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-02sc-multivariable-calculus-fall-2010/) | Multivariable calculus | Select partial derivatives, chain rule and gradients; defer most vector calculus |
| [Harvard Stat 110 practice](https://stat110.hsites.harvard.edu/strategic-practice-problems) | Probability exercises with solutions | Topic-labelled practice followed by homework combining unidentified concepts; select manageable problems |
| [Mathematics for Machine Learning](https://mml-book.github.io/) | Reference and bridge to ML | Geometry, decompositions, calculus, probability, optimisation and model examples; compact presentation requires prerequisite scaffolding |
| [Stanford CS336, Spring 2026](https://cs336.stanford.edu/) | Check relevance to modern language-model work | Current prerequisites include calculus, linear algebra, probability/statistics plus programming and deep learning; advanced destination, not our starting course |

The MML author site provides a freely available book PDF and additional exercises. Link to the author source; do not copy textbooks or answer banks into this repository.

## What the comparison changed

- Made geometry, projections and selected decompositions explicit.
- Added numerical reasoning, scaling and unstable calculations.
- Included likelihood, logs and introductory entropy to connect probability to learning objectives.
- Distinguished probability from statistical inference and evaluation.
- Adopted both topic-focused practice and mixed, unlabelled problems.
- Reduced architecture coverage to protect mathematical understanding.

The 100-day schedule, pacing, 80/20 time allocation and assessment gates are our design judgements. They are not endorsed by these institutions. Older foundational courses remain useful; the 2026 check establishes relevance rather than claiming newly published mathematics.

## Exercise provenance

For each external exercise, record source URL, course, section/problem identifier, access date and adaptations. Link rather than reproduce substantial source content. Label our original exercises and synthetic examples. Keep instructor solutions separate from learner attempts.

## Deliberate limits

This programme selects from multiple university courses; it does not complete them. Thorough independent understanding of every topic cannot be guaranteed by a calendar deadline. Use diagnostic evidence to repair prerequisites and trim selected/optional depth.
