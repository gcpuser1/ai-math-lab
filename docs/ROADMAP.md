# 100-session roadmap

Session numbers are provisional learning units. Replace optional breadth with prerequisite repair as needed.

## Daily evidence

Ordinary sessions: one small calculation, one explanation/derivation and one application or changed-assumption question. Explain back. Mixed sessions: select methods without topic labels. Gates: fresh problems under ASSESSMENT.md. Exact briefs are authored as learning progresses; this is not a pre-solved workbook.

Each 7-session group includes application/mixed work and review; adapt placement around explicit gates. Schedule delayed transfer approximately 7 days after gates, including after session 100. The session after a gate begins with any immediate repair.

## Application rotation

Latency and scaling in foundations; search, fitting and compression in linear algebra; sensitivity and learning in calculus; alerts and experiments in probability/statistics; training failures in optimisation. Reuse applications when the mathematics deepens.

## Weekly review

Record the strongest evidence, most consequential mistake, unresolved assumption, an independent transfer problem and any scope changes. Time and streaks are context, not mastery evidence.
